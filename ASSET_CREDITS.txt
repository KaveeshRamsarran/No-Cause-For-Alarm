NO CAUSE FOR ALARM - asset provenance (1.1)

School assets - A.R.S|T.
https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/school-assets-146253
Free, Standard Unity Asset Store EULA. Downloaded via the owner's account.
Used: school/office desks and chairs, cafeteria tables and seats, sink counters,
display counters, lockers, shelving, notice boards, books, computers and fire equipment.
Converted palette materials to URP, normalized dimensions and chair facing.

City People FREE Samples - Denys Almaral
https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/characters/city-people-free-samples-260446
Free, Standard Unity Asset Store EULA.
Used: nine adult weighted meshes for twelve roles, palette variants, authored
male/female idle, walk, jogging, alternate idle and self-check clips.
The ceiling creature is a distorted City People character. No priest is used.

VINTAGE LIVING ROOM 3D GAME PACK - ZNS3D
https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/vintage-living-room-3d-game-pack-314464
Free, Standard Unity Asset Store EULA.
Used: modular walls, floors, ceilings, windows, doors/frames and curtains.
Converted HDRP textures/materials to URP, normalized to the campus collision shell.
Furniture from this pack is not used in the current school.

The Unity Store sources and their derived prefabs/clips remain local and are
excluded from public source control. They are incorporated into the compiled game.
Free store assets remain subject to https://unity.com/legal/as-terms.
Restore through your own My Assets downloads and Tools/setup_graphics.ps1.

Original project content
Campus layout/collision, original supply props, animated flame shader, creature
adjustments, effects, UI, narrative, dialogue and game code were made for this project.
Original ambience, drone and fallback effects: Tools/generate_audio.py, synthesized PCM.
PA announcements: original dialogue rendered by local Windows System.Speech.
Historical 1.0 portrait mesh and AI-generated face atlas remain in source history;
the current cast uses the full City People heads, not that atlas.
No characters, textures, music, logos or dialogue from the reference game were copied.
No paid assets were purchased.
